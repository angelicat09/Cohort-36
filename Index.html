About Me

Hello! My name is Angie and I am a native to San Francisco. As a child, I loved taking public transportation and now as an adult, I love the accessibility of it throughout the city. Because of San Francisco's great transportation system, I am able to go anywhere, the possibilities are endless in San Francisco. My favorite food at the moment is Thai food. It continously changes, last year my favorite food was Vietmanese. I hope to continue to explore San Francisco and all of the great food it has to offer!
